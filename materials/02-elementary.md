## BÖLÜM 1: IŞ WE HÜNÄRLER
*Work & Jobs*

Bu bölümde hünärler, iş ýeri we gündelik iş tertibi barada Iňlisçe gürrüň etmek öwrenilýär.


### Sapak 1.1: Hünärler we kärler
*(Jobs and Professions)*

**Giriş**
Işiňiz we hünäriňiz barada gürrüň etmek gündelik durmuşda gaty möhümdir. Bu sapakda esasy hünär atlary, işiň ýeri we iş tertibi barada Iňlisçe öwrenersiňiz.

**Hünärler**

| Iňlisçe | Türkmençe | Dil bölegi |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| doctor | lukman | noun |
| engineer | inžener | noun |
| teacher | mugallym | noun |
| nurse | meditsinaçy / şepagat uýasy | noun |
| chef / cook | aşpez | noun |
| driver | sürüji | noun |
| businessman | telekeçi / işewür adam | noun |
| police officer | polisiýa işgäri | noun |

*Mysallar:*
- She is a doctor at the hospital. → Ol hassahanada lukman.
- My father is an engineer. → Meniň kakam inžener.
- He teaches English at school. → Ol mekdepde Iňlis dilini öwredýär.
- The nurse takes care of patients. → Şepagat uýasy näsaglara seredýär.
- The chef prepares delicious food. → Aşpez lezzetli nahar taýýarlaýar.
- He works as a taxi driver. → Ol taksi sürüji bolup işleýär.
- She is a successful businesswoman. → Ol üstünlikli telekeçi aýal.
- The police officer helps people. → Polisiýa işgäri adamlara kömek edýär.

**Söhbetdeşlik: Işiňiz näme?**

> **Merdan:** What do you do for work, Leýla?
> *(Leýla, siz nähili işleýärsiňiz?)*
> **Leýla:** I'm a nurse at the central hospital. I work with patients every day.
> *(Men merkezi hassahanada şepagat uýasy. Men her gün näsaglar bilen işleýärin.)*
> **Merdan:** That's a very important job! Do you enjoy it?
> *(Bu gaty möhüm iş! Siz muny halaýarsyňyzmy?)*
> **Leýla:** Yes, I love helping people. What about you?
> *(Hawa, men adamlara kömek etmegi söýýärin. Siziň işiňiz näme?)*
> **Merdan:** I'm an engineer. I design buildings.
> *(Men inžener. Men binalar taslamaýaryn.)*

**📖 Grammatika: Simple Present — iş üçin**
Hünäriňiz ýa-da işiňiz barada gürrüň etkeniňizde, hemişe Simple Present ulanylýar. 'I work as a...' ýa-da 'I am a...' gurluşlary iň köp ulanylýan usullardyr.

Gurluşy: `I work as a [hünär]. / I am a [hünär]. / She works at [iş ýeri].`

Mysallar:
- I work as a teacher in a school. → *Men mekdepde mugallym bolup işleýärin.*
- He works at a hospital. → *Ol hassahanada işleýär.*
- She starts work at 8 am. → *Ol işi sagat 8-de başlaýar.*


### Sapak 1.2: Iş ýeri we iş tertibi
*(Workplace and Work Routines)*

**Iş ýeri sözlügi**

|---------|-----------|-----------|
| office | ofis / edaraý | noun |
| meeting | duşuşyk / maslahat | noun |
| colleague | kärdeş / iş ýoldaşy | noun |
| salary | aýlyk / zähmet haky | noun |
| boss / manager | ýolbaşçy / müdir | noun |
| overtime | artyk wagt / ygtyýardan daşary wagt | noun |

*Mysallar:*
- I work in an office in the city centre. → Men şäher merkezindäki ofisde işleýärin.
- We have a meeting every Monday. → Biziň her duşenbe günü maslahatymyz bolýar.
- My colleagues are very friendly. → Meniň kärdeşlerim gaty mylakatly.

**📖 Grammatika: How often...? — Ýygylygy soramak**
'How often' soragy ýygylyk aňladýan jogaplary çaklaýar: always, usually, sometimes, rarely, never, every day/week/month. Jogapda adatça ýygylygy aňladýan söz ulanylýar.

Gurluşy: `How often do you [işlik]? — I [always/sometimes/...] + [işlik].`

Mysallar:
- How often do you have meetings? — We have them every Monday. → *Maslahatlar näçe gezek bolýar? — Her duşenbe günü.*
- Do you ever work overtime? — Yes, sometimes. → *Artyk wagt işleýärsiňizmi? — Hawa, käwagt.*

### Sapak 2.2: Geçen zaman — düzgünli işlikler
*(Past Simple — Regular Verbs)*

**📖 Grammatika: Past Simple (Geçen zaman ýönekeý)**
Geçen zaman geçmişde bolan we tamamlanan işleri beýan etmek üçin ulanylýar. Düzgünli işliklerde soňuna '-ed' goşulýar. Inkär: 'did not' (didn't) + işligiň esasy görnüşi. Sorag: 'Did + şahs + işlik?'

Gurluşy: `işlik + -ed | did not + işlik | Did + şahs + işlik?`

Mysallar:
- I visited Ashgabat last year. → *Men geçen ýyl Aşgabada gitdim.*
- She didn't travel last summer. → *Ol geçen tomusda syýahat etmedi.*
- Did you enjoy the trip? → *Syýahatyňyz hoş boldumy?*

**Geçen zaman — düzgünli işlikler**

| Iňlisçe | Türkmençe | Dil bölegi |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| visited (visit) | gitdi / baryp gördi | verb |
| stayed (stay) | galdy | verb |
| enjoyed (enjoy) | hezil etdi / hoşal boldy | verb |
| arrived (arrive) | geldi / ýetdi | verb |
| travelled (travel) | syýahat etdi | verb |

*Mysallar:*
- We visited the museum. → Biz muzeýe gitdik.
- They stayed at a hotel. → Olar myhmanhanada galdy.
- I enjoyed the food very much. → Men nahardan gaty hezil etdim.
- We arrived late at night. → Biz giç agşam geldik.
- She travelled by train. → Ol otly bilen syýahat etdi.

**Geçen zaman — mysallar**

- Last year I travelled to Turkey.
  *(Geçen ýyl men Türkiýä syýahat etdim.)*
- We stayed in a small hotel near the beach.
  *(Biz kenar ýakasyndaky kiçi myhmanhanada galdyk.)*
- Did you visit the old city? — Yes, we did!
  *(Köne şähere gitdiňizmi? — Hawa, gitdik!)*

## BÖLÜM 4: TEBIGAT WE DAŞKY GURŞAW
*Nature & Environment*

Bu bölümde tebigatyň beýany, deňeşdirme derejesi we daşky gurşaw öwrenilýär.


### Sapak 4.1: Tebigat we gözel ýerler
*(Nature and Landscape)*

**Tebigat sözlügi**

| Iňlisçe | Türkmençe | Dil bölegi |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| mountain | dag | noun |
| river | derýa / çaý | noun |
| desert | çöl | noun |
| forest | tokaý | noun |
| ocean / sea | okean / deňiz | noun |
| pollution | hapalama / azyk gurşawyň zaýalanmagy | noun |
| environment | daşky gurşaw / tebigat | noun |

*Mysallar:*
- The Kopet Dag mountains are beautiful. → Köpetdag daglary ajaýyp.
- The Amu Darya is a long river. → Amyderýa uzyn derýadyr.
- The Karakum desert is very hot. → Garagum çöli gaty yssydyr.
- There are many animals in the forest. → Tokaýda köp haýwan bar.
- The Caspian Sea is not an ocean. → Hazar deňzi okean däldir.
- Air pollution is a serious problem. → Howa hapalanmasy çynlakaý meseledir.
- We must protect the environment. → Biz daşky gurşawy goramaly.

**📖 Grammatika: Deňeşdirme — Comparatives**
Iki zady deňeşdirmek üçin comparatives ulanylýar. Gysga sypatlara '-er' goşulýar (tall → taller). Uzyn sypatlara 'more' goşulýar (beautiful → more beautiful). 'Than' sözi deňeşdirme söz birikmesini doldurýar.

Gurluşy: `[gysga sypat]-er than | more + [uzyn sypat] + than`

Mysallar:
- The mountain is taller than the hill. → *Dag depeden belentdir.*
- The Caspian Sea is bigger than I expected. → *Hazar deňzi meniň garaşanymdan ulydyr.*
- This desert is hotter than the other one. → *Bu çöl beýlekisinden has yssydyr.*


### Sapak 4.2: Iň üstün dereje
*(Superlatives)*

**📖 Grammatika: Superlatives — Iň üstün dereje**
Üç ýa-da has köp zady deňeşdirip, biriniň iň öňdedigini aýtmak üçin superlatives ulanylýar. Gysga sypatlara 'the + -est' goşulýar. Uzyn sypatlara 'the most + sypat' ulanylýar.

Gurluşy: `the + [gysga sypat]-est | the most + [uzyn sypat]`

Mysallar:
- The Everest is the highest mountain in the world. → *Everest dünýäde iň belent dagdyr.*
- The Amazon is the longest river. → *Amazon iň uzyn derýadyr.*
- This is the most beautiful place I have seen. → *Bu men gören ýerleriň iň owadanydyr.*

**Söhbetdeşlik: Ýurtlar barada**

> **Maral:** Which country do you think is the most beautiful?
> *(Siziňçe haýsy ýurt iň owadan?)*
> **Batyr:** I think Turkmenistan is one of the most beautiful! The Karakum desert is amazing.
> *(Menimçe Türkmenistan iň owadanlarynyň biri! Garagum çöli ajaýyp.)*
> **Maral:** I agree! And our mountains are the tallest in Central Asia.
> *(Ylalaşýaryn! We daglarymyz Orta Aziýanyň iň belentleridir.)*

## BÖLÜM 5: MEDENIÝET WE DÄP-DESSURLAR
*Culture & Traditions*

Bu bölümde Türkmen we dünýä medeniýeti barada gürrüňdeşlik we geçen zaman düzgünsiz işlikler öwrenilýär.


### Sapak 5.1: Baýramlar we däp-dessurlar
*(Festivals and Traditions)*

**Medeniýet we däp-dessur sözlügi**

| Iňlisçe | Türkmençe | Dil bölegi |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| festival | baýram / festival | noun |
| tradition | däp / dessur | noun |
| celebrate | baýramçylyk geçirmek / bellemek | verb |
| costume | milli egin-eşik / geýim | noun |
| dance | tans / küştdepdi | noun |
| music | saz / aýdym-saz | noun |

*Mysallar:*
- Nowruz is a spring festival. → Nowruz ýaz baýramydyr.
- This is an old tradition in our culture. → Bu biziň medeniýetimizdäki köne däpdir.
- We celebrate Nowruz every March. → Biz her mart aýynda Nowruzy belleýäris.
- She wore a traditional costume. → Ol milli egin-eşik geýdi.
- Traditional dances are beautiful. → Milli tanslar ajaýypdyr.
- Turkmen music is unique. → Türkmen sazynyň özboluşly sesi bar.

**📖 Grammatika: Past Simple — Düzgünsiz işlikler**
Käbir Iňlis işlikleri geçen zamanda '-ed' goşulmasy bilen üýtgemeýär — olar düzgünsiz işlikler. Olary ýat tutmaly! Iň köp ulanylýan düzgünsiz işlikler: go→went, eat→ate, see→saw, come→came, take→took, make→made.

Gurluşy: `go → went | eat → ate | see → saw | come → came | take → took`

Mysallar:
- We went to the Nowruz festival. → *Biz Nowruz baýramyna gitdik.*
- I saw a wonderful traditional dance. → *Men ajaýyp milli tans gördüm.*
- She wore a beautiful costume. → *Ol owadan egin-eşik geýdi.*


### Sapak 5.2: Has köp düzgünsiz işlikler

|---------|-----------|-----------|

| bought (buy) | satyn aldy | verb |
| wrote (write) | ýazdy | verb |
| spoke (speak) | gürledi / gepledi | verb |
| thought (think) | pikir etdi | verb |
| knew (know) | bildi | verb |

*Mysallar:*
- She gave me a gift. → Ol maňa sowgat berdi.
- He bought a new car. → Ol täze maşyn satyn aldy.
- I wrote a letter. → Men hat ýazdym.
- She spoke English well. → Ol Iňlisçe gowy gürledi.
- I thought about it all day. → Men bütin gün bu barada pikir etdim.
- He knew the answer. → Ol jogaby bildi.

**Söhbetdeşlik: Nowruz baýramy**

> **Gurban:** How did you celebrate Nowruz this year?
